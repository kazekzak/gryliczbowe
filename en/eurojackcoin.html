EuroJackCoin

An original, educational cryptocurrency and peer-to-peer network written from scratch in C++ — its own blockchain, wallets with Ed25519 keys, mining, and transaction propagation between nodes on the network.

An educational project — built to learn how blockchain works from the inside. You can't exchange EJC for real money (or buy a coffee with it) — but you'll mine your own blockchain from scratch and know exactly where every "cent" in this system comes from. Satisfaction guaranteed.
