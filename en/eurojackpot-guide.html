An important note first

Eurojackpot draws are independent of each other. No method, statistic or simulation in this app increases your chance of winning. Statistics and simulations only show the history of past draws and are for learning and fun – they are not a forecast.

The app does not sell tickets and does not accept bets. Saved “bets” are just notes on your phone.

1. Getting around the app

Main screen

  • Balls at the top – touch the header and the balls spin. It is a plain random generator (5 from 50 and 2 from 12) with no analysis at all. You cannot save the result.
  • “draws” – the history of draws in the database. Touch a draw and you will see three comparisons: with the whole history, with your saved bets and with your sent bets.
  • “Update” – downloads missing, newer draws from the internet (Premium; in the free version you can only load the built-in database). A red dot appears when the database has not been brought up to date since the most recent Tuesday or Friday (draw days); this check is based on the calendar only, without the internet.
  • Flags – change the language (PL, DE, EN, FR, IT). The app restarts in the new language.
  • The “?” icon – description of the app, version and disclaimer.

2. How the statistics are calculated (ANALYSIS tab)

Time windows

Most screens have the buttons 50 / 100 / 200 / All. They mean “the last N draws”, counted from the newest (draws, not days). “All” is the whole database.

Euro numbers and rule changes

The pool of Euro numbers has changed over time: until 9 October 2014 it was 1–8, until 24 March 2022 it was 1–10, and from 25 March 2022 it is 1–12. So that numbers 9–12 are not undercounted, Euro statistics are calculated only from draws with the same range as the newest one (today: from 25 March 2022).

Hot / Cold – four methods

The screen shows a grid of numbers 1–50 and Euro 1–12 in colors. You can select numbers (up to 5 main and 2 Euro) and save a bet. There are four ways of calculating the “score” of each number:

  1. Frequency – how many times the number was drawn in the chosen window.
  2. Weighted – newer draws count more. The weight is linear: the newest draw has a weight equal to the number of draws in the window (e.g. 100), the oldest has a weight of 1. The score is the sum of the weights of the draws in which the number was drawn. Example (window 100): the number was drawn in the draws numbered 0, 10, 50 and 99 from the end → 100 + 90 + 50 + 1 = 241.
  3. Window – only the newest 1/3 of the chosen window counts (for 50 / 100 / 200 that is the last 16 / 33 / 66 draws).
  4. Percentile – the percentage of numbers whose frequency was no greater than this number's (the most frequent one has 100%). It gives the same ranking as Frequency, just expressed in percent.

Colors: hot (red) – the 10 main numbers with the highest score, warm (orange) – the next 10, cold (blue) – the 10 with the lowest. For Euro: 2 hot, 2 warm, 2 cold. With ties, a few more numbers may be colored.

Overdue numbers

For each number: overdue = how many draws have passed since its last appearance (0 = drawn in the latest draw) and average gap = the average distance between its consecutive appearances in the window. The list is sorted from the longest absent. Row color: red – absent for at least 30% of the window, orange – at least 15%, green – at most 3%, the rest gray.

Time trends

Compares two adjacent windows of N draws (25 / 50 / 100 / 200): the last N and the previous N. For each number it calculates the difference in share: (appearances in the last N / N) − (appearances in the previous N / N). Growing when the difference exceeds +2 percentage points, declining when it is below −2, and stable in all other cases. Example (N = 50): 8 times in the last 50 and 4 times in the previous 50 = 16% vs 8% → +8 points, a growing trend.

Number pairs

In each draw it counts all 10 pairs of main numbers and shows those that occurred together most often. You can move a ready pair (a basket of up to 5 numbers) to “Custom numbers”.

Correlations

Shows which pairs of numbers are drawn together more or less often than chance alone would suggest. The measure is lift: lift = (number of times the pair was drawn together × number of draws) / (frequency of number A × frequency of number B). Lift 1 means “just as with independence”. Example: A was drawn 10 times, B 8 times, and together 3 times in 100 draws → lift = 3 × 100 / (10 × 8) = 3.75. With small counts, lift is mostly chance – that is why the “Strongest correlations” list includes only pairs that were drawn together at least 2 times.

Sequences

You choose one number. The app finds the draws in which it was drawn and checks which numbers were drawn in the draw immediately after each of them. It shows the 20 most frequent: how many times and what percentage. For comparison: with pure chance, each main number is drawn in about 10% of draws (5/50), and each Euro number in about 16.7% (2/12, since 2022). The newest draw is not counted, because it does not have a following draw yet.

Cross analysis

You combine two analyses: A and B (Hot, Cold, Overdue, Trends ↑, Trends ↓). The app shows three sets: the intersection, only A and only B. It is plain set arithmetic, with no scoring. From each set you can make a bet with the “Generate” button (a random draw from the pool). Note: “overdue” on this screen means a number whose overdue count has exceeded its own average gap (at least 5 draws), and the 50/100/200 ranges use a double window, because the “last” and “previous” draws are compared; that is why the header shows e.g. “50+50 draws”.

Number sums

For each draw, the sum of the 5 main numbers is calculated. The screen shows the minimum, maximum, average, median, a histogram in steps of 10 (e.g. 120–129) and the 15 most common sums. The marked range is the values from the 20th to the 80th percentile, i.e. roughly the middle 60% of draws. For comparison: the average sum of 5 numbers out of 50 is 127.5.

Structure analysis

  • Even vs Odd – how many draws had 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 even numbers. With pure chance, the most common patterns are 2E+3O and 3E+2O (about 32.6% each), while the extremes 0E and 5E are rare (about 2.5% each).
  • Number Range – the difference between the largest and the smallest number in a draw, with a histogram in steps of 5.

Predictive analysis (neural network)

A small neural network (124 inputs → 40 neurons → 62 outputs: 50 main numbers + 12 Euro). For each number, the input is two features from recent draws: frequency and “freshness” (how long ago it was drawn). The network is trained from scratch on every run, on the selected range (max. 120 epochs), so the result may differ between runs. “Score” is a ranking of similarity to patterns from history, not a probability. The screen also shows the training and validation error, so you can judge whether the network learns anything beyond plain frequency.

3. How the bet generators work (GAMES tab)

Hit or Miss – 5 modes

You choose a mode, the number of recent draws to analyze (20 / 50 / 100 / 200 / all) and the number of sets (1 / 3 / 5 / 10 / 20; sets of 5, 10 and 20 are Premium), then “GENERATE”. Each bet has a save icon.

  • RANDOM – every number has an equal chance (5 from 50 + 2 from 12).
  • HOT – draws from numbers more frequent than average; the more frequent the number, the greater its weight.
  • COLD – draws from numbers rarer than average; the rarer the number (including one not drawn at all in the window), the greater its weight (weight = 1 / (frequency + 1)).
  • OVERDUE – for each number, score = (overdue + 1) / (frequency + 1). From the 15 main numbers (7 Euro) with the highest score, numbers are drawn with a weight proportional to the score.
  • SYNTHESIS – a mix: the sets are split in the proportion 40% hot, 30% cold, 30% overdue (e.g. 5 sets = 2 hot + 2 cold + 1 overdue), arranged in blocks in that order.

Note: the description of “cold” in the app says “numbers that haven't been drawn for a long time”, but what is actually counted is low frequency in the window. “Overdue” is a separate method, based on the time since the last appearance.

Custom numbers

You choose main numbers and Euro numbers, and then you save the bet or tap “Analyze”. “Analyze” does not save anything. With a complete bet (5 main + 2 Euro) it shows how it would have done in the whole history. With a smaller selection (e.g. 3 main numbers + 1 Euro) it opens a separate analysis of your numbers (described below).

Analysis of your numbers (partial selection)

Select any set (e.g. 3 main numbers and 1 Euro) and tap “Analyze”. Only prize-paying layouts are included in the analysis, that is, those that win a prize in EuroJackpot – the 12 prize tiers: 5+2, 5+1, 5+0, 4+2, 4+1, 4+0, 3+2, 2+2, 3+1, 3+0, 1+2 and 2+1 (the first number is main numbers, the second is Euro). A layout such as 2+0 wins nothing, so the app shows a message that it is not included in the analysis.

The screen shows:

  • Together in one draw – how many times all selected numbers were drawn together, when last, and, for comparison, how many times you would expect that by pure chance.
  • Each number separately – in how many draws it appeared and when last. For Euro numbers only the draws in which the number could be drawn are counted, because the Euro pool changed over time (1–8, from 10.10.2014 1–10, from 25.03.2022 1–12).
  • Combinations of your numbers – lines grouped by prize tier, from the highest (e.g. “4+1 · four numbers + one euro”). A line is one layout: balls of the main numbers, possibly Euro, and the number of draws in which it came out. Lines with a result above zero are clickable – they open the list of draws with dates and the gaps between them.
  • Most frequent companions – numbers drawn most often in draws containing your whole combination (with a small sample this has little value).
  • Draws with this combination – the list of draws, or a note that the combination has never been drawn in full.

This only describes history: draws are independent, no method increases your chance of winning, and the fact that a combination came out once or never says nothing about the next draw.

Favorite numbers

You mark up to 4 main numbers and 1 Euro number that should be in every set. The app fills in the rest: randomly, from the 10 hottest or the 10 coldest numbers in the chosen window (for Euro: 5). You choose the window (50 / 100 / 200 / 500 / all) and the number of bets (1 / 3 / 5 / 10 / 20). In the free version, the window of 50 and 1 or 3 bets are available; bigger windows and more bets are Premium.

System play

You choose 6–10 main numbers and 2 Euro numbers. A full system is all combinations of 5 numbers from your n (6 numbers = 6 bets, 7 = 21, 8 = 56, 9 = 126, 10 = 252). A short system takes roughly every third combination from the list (8 numbers = 18 bets, 9 = 42, 10 = 84). It is just an even sample, and not a system with guaranteed hits. All combinations get the same 2 Euro numbers.

Stat. broker – 5 profiles

For each number from 1 to 50, the app calculates a score that depends on the profile, builds a ranking and splits it: positions 1–15 are “hot”, 16–30 are “warm”, 31–50 are “cold”. Each bet is 3 random numbers from the hot ones + 1 from the warm ones + 1 from the cold ones. The profiles differ only in the formula for the score:

  • Safe – score = frequency.
  • Balanced – frequency × multiplier (0.85 for the 10 most frequent, 1.10 for positions 11–25, 1.0 for the rest).
  • Aggressive – overdue × 4 − frequency × 0.7 (favors the numbers unseen for the longest time).
  • Predictive – frequency × 2.5 + 80 / (overdue + 1) + 30 / (average gap + 1) (favors numbers that were drawn recently).
  • Random Forest – 5 “trees” vote, i.e. 5 rankings: window 50 (frequency), window 100 (frequency), window 100 (overdue), window 200 (frequency), window 200 (overdue). Each one gives a vote to its 15 best numbers. The screen shows “x/5 trees”. This is not a classic, trained random forest, just a vote of several simple rankings; with a small range (50 or 100) some of the trees are identical.

Euro numbers are chosen by frequency (1 of the 5 most frequent + 1 of the next 4); in Random Forest also by voting.

My bets, Sent bets, Coupon scanner

  • My bets – saved bets (newest first). Touching one shows the analysis of the bet in the history. The ticket button marks the bet as purchased and moves it to Sent bets. Deleting bets, “Delete all”, the ticket button and opening the analysis from this list are Premium (without Premium you can also open a bet's analysis from “Historical test” or “Custom numbers → Analyze”).
  • Sent bets – your purchased tickets. You can compare them with a draw on the “draws” screen. Deleting (including “Delete all”) is Premium; bets get onto this list without Premium through the Coupon scanner.
  • Coupon scanner – see below. The app warns about duplicates (the same set of numbers already in the database).

Coupon scanner step by step

  1. Open the “Coupon scanner” tile and tap “Scan with camera” (the first time, you grant permission for the camera; without it, “From gallery” works).
  2. Show the whole coupon to the camera: the numbers and the draw date. Hold it still, in good light, from a distance of about 10–20 cm (4–8 inches).
  3. The app reads the text on your phone (the image is not sent or saved). The bet saves itself to “Sent bets” when 6 consecutive frames give exactly the same, valid reading (5 numbers + 2 Euro) together with the draw date. You will hear a beep and feel a vibration.
  4. Choose “Scan next” or “Finish”.
  5. The app reads only the numbers of the bet and the draw date; serial numbers and barcodes are ignored. A bet with the same date that has 4 or more numbers in common with one already saved is rejected as a probable misreading of the same coupon. System coupons (more than 5 numbers in a line) are not supported. You can correct the numbers manually, and after the draw the green numbers show matches.

4. Simulations (TESTS tab)

Historical test

You choose a saved bet, and the app checks it against all draws in the database: how many times it would have landed in each of the 12 prize categories (see below), what the average and the longest gap between hits was, and when the last time was. Note: in the past the Euro pool was smaller, so bets with Euro numbers 9–12 had lower chances in older draws.

Monte Carlo

For the chosen bet, the app simulates 1,000, 10,000 or 100,000 completely random draws (under today's rules) and counts how many times the bet would have hit each category. The result is “Probability” and “1 win per…”. Every bet has exactly the same chances, so differences between bets are just chance. With rare categories (e.g. 5+2), the result will almost always be 0.

Backtest

Checks how five methods would have done on older draws: Hot, Cold, Overdue, Scoring and Neural. You set the “Knowledge base” (from which draw to which draw the method learns) and “How many draws to test” after it.

  • The four fast methods build one set from the knowledge base and check it against all the following test draws. Scoring = 0.45 × frequency + 0.30 × freshness + 0.25 × trend (each component scaled to 0–1).
  • Neural trains the network from scratch for every test draw, using only older data (a fair “step by step” test), so the Neural results are not fully comparable with the others.
  • The table shows how many times the method hit 5+2, 4+X, 3+X, 2+X, and the points – an internal comparison scale (5+2 = 10,000 … 2+1 = 5), not real prize amounts.
  • “Suggestion for the next draw” is the bet of the winning method for the draw that comes right after the test window.

Backtest vs random players (Premium)

It answers the question “Does the method beat chance?”. Six methods (Hot (all history), Hot (last 50), Cold (all history), Cold (last 50), Overdue, Repeat last) pick one bet for each draw from the 101st to the last, using only earlier draws. At the same time, 1,000 simulated players pick random bets. For each method, a percentile is calculated: the percentage of random players who had fewer wins (50% = exactly the level of chance). A win is any of the 12 categories. The random seed is fixed, so the result is repeatable for the same database. When several methods are tested, one or two fall outside the 5–95% range by pure chance.

6. Free version and Premium

Most of the app works for free. Premium is a subscription bought on Google Play (monthly or yearly; the price is shown on the Premium screen in your currency). You can cancel the subscription at any time in Google Play, and the app has no access to your card details.

What Premium gives you:

  • downloading new draws from the internet (“Update”),
  • no ads,
  • saving bets without a limit (in the free version you can have at most 20 saved bets),
  • deleting saved bets,
  • more sets at once in the generators and longer analysis windows in Favorite numbers,
  • the “Backtest vs random players” screen.

What works for free: the whole ANALYSIS tab, Stat. broker, System play, Custom numbers, Coupon scanner, Monte Carlo, Historical test, Predictive analysis, Backtest, the comparisons from the “draws” screen and loading the built-in database. The free version also shows ads.

The limit of 20 bets applies to saved bets that have not been sent yet. Bets added by the scanner go straight to “Sent bets”, so they do not count toward this limit. Without Premium you cannot delete bets, so the slots taken by saved bets cannot be freed up.

Where you will meet Premium: sets of 5 / 10 / 20 in Hit or Miss; bigger windows and more bets in Favorite numbers; deleting in “My bets” and “Sent bets” and the ticket button; “Download new” in the database update; the whole “Backtest vs random” screen.

7. Frequently asked questions

Does any method increase the chance of winning? No. Draws are independent, and every combination has exactly the same chance. The methods show the history and help you understand it.

Why does the neural network show slightly different numbers every time? It is trained from scratch with a random start, so the result is not repeatable.

Does the scanner send photos of coupons? No. Reading happens on your phone, and the image is neither saved nor sent.

Where does the draw data come from? The app has a built-in database (up to April 2026) and can download missing, newer draws from a file on the internet (a Premium feature).

Do I have to buy Premium to use the analyses? No. All the analyses in the ANALYSIS tab are free. Premium adds the database update, no ads, saving without a limit, deleting bets and a few extensions described in section 6.

A final note

Remember: draws are independent, and no method increases your chance of winning. Statistics and simulations describe the past – play responsibly and only for fun.
