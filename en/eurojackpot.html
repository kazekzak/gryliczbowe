EurojackpotApp

An advanced Android mobile app for statistical analysis of the EuroJackpot lottery (5 of 50 + 2 of 12). Includes a database of over 970 historical draws (updatable) and works fully offline.

📊 Statistical Analysis

  • Hot and cold numbers — 4 calculation methods: frequency, weighted, time window, percentile
  • Overdue numbers — the ones absent the longest from draws
  • Trends — numbers gaining or losing popularity
  • Successors — analysis of the number drawn after a given number
  • Correlations — pairs of numbers that appear together
  • Cross analysis — combine any two analysis methods at once
  • Sums, parity structures, neural network

🎮 Bet Generation

  • Strategies: Hot, Cold, Overdue, Synthesis, Random
  • Full and reduced systems (C(n,5) combinations)
  • Guaranteed picks — force your favorite numbers
  • Custom numbers — enter your own
  • Statistical Broker — 5 profiles: safe, balanced, aggressive, predictive, and Random Forest (voting of several independent methods)

🔬 Simulations

  • Monte Carlo — simulation of millions of draws
  • Backtest — historical evaluation of method effectiveness, with precise range tuning
  • AI Scoring — prediction with weighted factors

📋 Bet Management

  • Save, history, and comparison of bets
  • Historical test — check how many hits you would have had in the past
  • History of 970+ EuroJackpot draws

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

1. Data Collection

The EurojackpotApp app does not collect, process, or transmit any personal user data to external servers.

2. Locally Stored Data

All data is stored exclusively on the user's device:

  • Saved bets and number selections
  • Historical database of EuroJackpot draws
  • App settings

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all this data.

3. Draw Database Update (Premium Feature)

The database update feature downloads a CSV file with draw results from a public internet source. The connection is made only at the user's request. No data identifying the user is collected during this connection.

4. In-App Purchases

The Premium subscription purchase is handled by Google Play. The app does not process or store payment data. Google Play's privacy policy is available at: policies.google.com/privacy

5. App Permissions

  • INTERNET — used exclusively for updating the draw database (Premium)

6. Planned Changes (Ads)

In an upcoming update, we plan to introduce ads (Google AdMob) in the free version of the app. Once that happens, this privacy policy will be updated and will specify what data (e.g. the device's advertising identifier) will then be processed by Google AdMob.
